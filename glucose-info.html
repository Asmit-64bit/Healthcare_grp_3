AI Colorimetry Note

Pad 1 on the 10-parameter dipstick utilizes glucose oxidase / peroxidase reaction changing from teal green to dark brown.

Reagent Pad 1: Glucose (Glu)

Clinical Significance

Glucose is normally absent in urine. Glycosuria occurs when blood glucose exceeds the renal threshold (approx. 180 mg/dL), commonly caused by uncontrolled Diabetes Mellitus, gestational diabetes, or renal tubule disorders.

Colorimetry Calibration Scale

Negative (0 mg/dL)
Trace (100 mg/dL)
1+ (250 mg/dL)
2+ (500 mg/dL)
3+ (1000+ mg/dL)

AI Reader Technical Specs (KJS-HC-03)

  • Reaction Time: Exactly 60 Seconds post-dip.
  • RGB Wavelength Sensitivity: Peak green/red channel reflectance ratio shift.
  • Linearity Range: 0 to 1000 mg/dL continuous numerical concentration.
