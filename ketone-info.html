Sodium Nitroprusside Reaction

Reacts primarily with acetoacetic acid in alkaline solution producing a color transition from buff-pink to maroon/purple.

Reagent Pad 4: Ketone (Ket)

Clinical Significance

Ketonuria occurs when the body metabolizes fat for energy instead of glucose. It is a vital indicator for Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA), severe carbohydrate starvation, prolonged fasting, or strenuous physical exertion.

Colorimetry Calibration Scale

Negative (0 mg/dL)
Trace (5 mg/dL)
Small (15 mg/dL)
Moderate (40 mg/dL)
Large (160+ mg/dL)

AI Reader Technical Specs (KJS-HC-03)

  • Reaction Time: 40 Seconds post-dip.
  • Sensitivity: 5 mg/dL acetoacetate limit of detection.
