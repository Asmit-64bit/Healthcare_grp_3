Key Diagnostic Terms Explained

CBC
Complete Blood Count evaluating Red Blood Cells, White Blood Cells, and Platelets.
HbA1c
Glycated Hemoglobin Test providing 3-month average blood glucose control for Diabetes.
LFT
Liver Function Test evaluating SGOT, SGPT, Bilirubin, and Serum Protein levels.
KFT
Kidney Function Test measuring Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen, and Electrolytes.
