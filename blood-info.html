Pseudoperoxidase Reaction

Detects intact red blood cells (speckled pattern) as well as free hemoglobin and myoglobin (uniform green/dark blue).

Reagent Pad 3: Blood / Hemoglobin (Blo)

Clinical Significance

Hematuria (presence of red blood cells or hemoglobin in urine) indicates potential kidney stones, urinary tract trauma, infection, glomerulonephritis, or bladder disorders.

Colorimetry Calibration Scale

Negative
Trace (Non-hemolyzed)
Small (10 RBC/µL)
Moderate (50 RBC/µL)
Large (250 RBC/µL)

AI Reader Technical Specs (KJS-HC-03)

  • Reaction Time: 60 Seconds post-dip.
  • Segmentation Detail: High-resolution spatial spot analysis to distinguish intact RBC speckles from uniform hemolyzed hemoglobin.
