Double Indicator System

Uses methyl red and bromothymol blue providing distinct color transitions from orange (pH 5.0) to green to dark blue (pH 8.5).

Reagent Pad 5: pH Level (pH)

Clinical Significance

Urine pH evaluates systemic acid-base balance and renal tubular secretion. Acidic urine (< 5.5) increases risk of uric acid stones; alkaline urine (> 7.5) may indicate urea-splitting bacterial UTIs or renal tubular acidosis.

Colorimetry Calibration Scale

pH 5.0 (Acidic)
pH 6.0 (Normal)
pH 7.0 (Neutral)
pH 8.0 (Alkaline)
pH 8.5 (High Alkaline)
