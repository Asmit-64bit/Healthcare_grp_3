Protein Error of Indicators

Based on tetrabromphenol blue pH indicator error, transitioning yellow (negative) to turquoise/blue green (positive albumin).

Reagent Pad 2: Protein (Pro)

Clinical Significance

Proteinuria (excess protein in urine) is a key early indicator of kidney disease, glomerulonephritis, diabetic nephropathy, preeclampsia in pregnancy, or severe urinary tract infection.

Colorimetry Calibration Scale

Negative (<15 mg/dL)
Trace (15-30 mg/dL)
1+ (30 mg/dL)
2+ (100 mg/dL)
3+ (300+ mg/dL)

AI Reader Technical Specs (KJS-HC-03)

  • Reaction Time: 60 Seconds post-dip.
  • Primary Analyte Sensitive: Serum Albumin.
  • Continuous Range: 0 to 500 mg/dL estimated concentration via polynomial regression model.
