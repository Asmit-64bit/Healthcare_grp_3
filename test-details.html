Complete Blood Count (CBC) - Test Details

Purpose: To evaluate overall health and detect a wide range of blood disorders, including anemia, infections, and leukemia.

Sample Required: 2 ml Venous Blood Sample in EDTA Tube.

Preparation Instructions: Overnight fasting (8-10 hours) is recommended for accurate metabolic parameters.

Normal Reference Range: Varies by age and gender (e.g., Male Hemoglobin: 13.8 to 17.2 g/dL, Female: 12.1 to 15.1 g/dL).

Report Delivery Time: Within 12 Hours.
